# Product

## Overview

- **Product Name:** PhantasmTrack (GrimCourier)
- **Category:** Costume Contest — a haunting, polished Dark-Fantasy Grimoire / Adventurer's Guild Log UI.

## Core Problem Solved

Cross-border online shoppers and small importers struggle to track multiple international packages, courier fees, and strict customs limits. A common example is the **4x4 rule**: a maximum of **4 kg** and **$400 USD FOB** per package, plus annual quota limits. Missing these thresholds leads to unexpected customs taxes or retained shipments.

## Solution

A gothic dark-fantasy grimoire where each package is an active **"Quest"** traversing arcane portals:

```
Origin Warehouse  ->  Customs Abyss  ->  Local Guild Delivery
```

Key capabilities:

- An automated **Customs & Tariff Alchemist Calculator** that computes taxes and fees.
- Visual **"Mana Bars"** that show how close a package is to its weight (kg) and budget ($) limits.

The experience should feel like consulting a living spellbook: atmospheric, polished, and legible, while keeping the underlying logistics information accurate and useful.
